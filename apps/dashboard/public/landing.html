FAQ

Common questions

Will my viewers know it's AI?

Only if you want them to. Every export is watermarked with ai_generated: true metadata for EU AI Act compliance, but that's invisible to viewers. We never inject a "Made by Vireo" watermark on your video unless you ask.

How is Vireo different from Opus Clip / Riverside / Descript?

They edit. We publish. Vireo is the only tool that closes the loop: Style DNA → Edit → Adapt → Publish → Measure → Learn. Your 7th publish makes your 8th publish better.

What about the EU AI Act?

Article 12 requires logging, watermarking, and explainability for all AI-generated content. We do all three by default. Non-compliance fines start at €15M or 3% of global revenue. Vireo keeps you out of that headline.

Can I use my own music?

Yes — upload your own tracks, or pick from our royalty-free library. We auto-duck the music under your voice using sidechain compression.

Do you clone other creators?

No. Vireo learns from YOUR content only. We don't train on other people's videos without permission. The "Style Transfer" feature in Business tier is for cloning your own voice, not someone else's.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contracts, no commitments. Cancel from your account page. Your Style DNA stays — you just can't publish new content while on Free.
